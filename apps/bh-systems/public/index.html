The problem you actually have

The demo was the easy part.

Most people who do AI can get a workflow to work once, in a demo. Almost nobody can make it run for every customer, every day, and tell you the moment it breaks instead of failing in the dark.

production.log

You've already built the AI. Matching, screening, parsing, a GPT-style tool, whatever it is. It works, you shipped it. But you've watched it do strange things in production:

warna screen quietly passes over good candidates
warna sync fails and nobody notices until a customer emails
warna job hangs for two minutes and you're not sure why

The hard part is making it run reliably without you watching it, and knowing the moment it breaks. You don't have an engineer who's scaled production systems, and you're not ready to put a $200K staff engineer on payroll to own it. That's the gap I fill.
