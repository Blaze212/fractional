INTERACTIVE FIELD NOTES CALL — SEMI-GUIDED SCRIPT
====================================================
Companion to the existing single-shot flow (see field-notes.xml /
sample-call-script.txt). Walks the adjuster through the Ibis template
section by section instead of one continuous narration. This is a
tool an adjuster uses ~15x/week — every line below is what actually
gets said on the call. No menu-reading, no "press 1 for yes, 2 for
no," no "if you don't have one, just say so." One short, direct
question per section; the adjuster answers the way they'd answer a
colleague, in whatever words fit, and the system figures out the
rest. If a rule needs explaining, it's a comment in this file, not a
sentence Telnyx says out loud.

VERB TAGS
----------
Each section is tagged with which TeXML verb captures it (see
docs/telnyx-texml-interactive-ivr.md for the full reasoning):

  RECORD    — plain narration, transcribed as-is, no live parsing
  GATHER    — one genuinely closed answer (a real 2–3 option set);
              speech or DTMF both accepted, but never read aloud —
              the adjuster just answers in words
  AIGATHER  — one flexible exchange that can resolve a branch, a
              free-text value, and/or a short follow-up detail all
              from a single natural answer, asking again only if
              something required is still missing

REQUIRED / SKIP-OK marks match enums.json's `required` flag.


0. CONTACT & INSURANCE INFO
------------------------------
[RECORD]
IVR: "Hi, you've called the claim line. Let's walk the Ibis template.
Who's the insured, and what's the property address?"

Adjuster: "Barton Holdridge, ten five oh three Waters Drive, Irving."

IVR: "Claim number and carrier?"

Adjuster: "C-L-M one one two two three three, State Farm."
  (system) — feeds the existing identity matcher (matcher.js): last
  name + street number/name + city + claim number. Not an Ibis
  template field itself — read the claim number back once for
  confirmation, since spoken alphanumerics are exactly what
  speech-to-text mangles.


1. ASSIGNMENT
--------------
[AIGATHER] — fields: contacted_party_name, present_at_inspection
IVR: "Who'd you make contact with to set this up, and who was at the
inspection?"

Adjuster: "Called Barton, that's the insured, to set it up. He met me
out there, had my guy with me too."
  (system) — present_at_inspection_verb (was/were) is derived from
  whether one person or several were named; never asked directly.

  NOTE: never ask for date received / contacted / inspected / date of
  loss anywhere in this call. Those render as the literal
  [DATE_RECEIVED] / [DATE_CONTACTED] / [DATE_INSPECTED] / [DATE_LOSS]
  tokens in the Ibis template — Ibis's own merge fields (README.md
  "Phase 1c") — asking and discarding the answer wastes the call.


2. MORTGAGE
------------
[AIGATHER] — fields: mortgage_status, mortgage_company (only if has one)
IVR: "Does the insured have a mortgage on the property?"

Adjuster: "No." — or — "Yeah, it's through Chase."
  (system) — one exchange resolves the branch and the company name
  together. No separate yes/no step, no follow-up question when the
  answer already contains everything.


3. ORIGIN
----------
[RECORD] — field: origin_narrative
IVR: "What happened — walk me through the cause of loss."

Adjuster: (free narration)


4. COVERAGE
------------
[AIGATHER] — fields: coverage_cause_narrative, coverage_determination,
             coverage_supporting_detail (optional)
IVR: "What's the coverage situation on this one?"

Adjuster: "It's a covered peril, no concerns from me." — or — "Burst
plumbing line, freeze related, covered — we confirmed heat was
maintained." — or — "Excluded, this one's a maintenance issue, not a
covered peril."
  (system) — this is how adjusters already say it in one breath (see
  sample-call-script.txt: "Nothing weird on coverage, it's a covered
  peril, no concerns from me."). One answer carries cause,
  determination, and supporting detail when there is one; determination
  still resolves to the exact covered/excluded template wording, it's
  just extracted from natural speech instead of asked as a separate
  choice.


5. RISK INFORMATION
---------------------
[AIGATHER] — fields: dwelling_type, dwelling_stories, foundation_type,
             square_footage, bedroom_count, bathroom_count, occupancy_status
IVR: "Give me the dwelling — stories, type, foundation, square
footage, bedrooms, bathrooms, and who's living there."

Adjuster: "One story ranch, single family, slab foundation, about
twenty-one fifty square feet, four bed two bath, owner occupied."
  (system) — dwelling_type/dwelling_stories/bedroom_count/bathroom_count
  are free text/number here, not a closed list — "apartment," "a story
  and a half," a 7-bedroom house are all real answers a fixed enum
  would reject. bedroom_count/bathroom_count parse to integers.
  foundation_type and occupancy_status stay genuinely closed (3 options
  each, no real edge cases seen). One AIGather turn re-asks only
  whatever's still missing once the adjuster stops talking — this is
  the section with the most required fields (7), so that matters.

IVR: "What's the siding, and roughly what year was it built?"

Adjuster: "Brick veneer, built in ninety-eight." — or — "Vinyl siding,
not sure on the year."
  (system) — siding_type free text with example guidance (same
  reasoning as dwelling_type); year_built [SKIP-OK].


6. DWELLING INSPECTION — ROOF
--------------------------------
[GATHER] — field: roof_status (branch)
IVR: "Roof — affected or not?"

Adjuster: "Not affected." → skip to section 7, nothing else renders.
Adjuster: "Yeah." / "It's a mess up there." → continue to 6a/6b.
  (system) — kept as a plain Gather, not folded into the AIGather
  below, because this branch decides which schema the next turn even
  uses (shingle vs. non-shingle vs. nothing) — it needs to resolve
  before the follow-up question can be built.

6a. (roof_status = shingle)
[AIGATHER] — fields: roof_covering_type, roof_age_years, roof_condition,
             roof_pitch, roof_damage_narrative
IVR: "Tell me about the roof — material, age, condition, pitch, and
what you found slope by slope, including anything with no damage."

Adjuster: (free narration covering all of it in one go, e.g. "Thirty
year architectural laminate, call it twelve years old, average
condition, six twelve pitch. Above the garage the decking and
shingles are burned straight through, twelve by fourteen...")
GO TO 7

6b. (roof_status = other_material)
[RECORD] — field: roof_narrative_freeform
IVR: "It's not shingle, so just describe the whole thing — material,
age, condition, layers, pitch, per-slope findings, and your
conclusion."

Adjuster: (free narration)
GO TO 7


7. DWELLING INSPECTION — EXTERIOR
------------------------------------
[AIGATHER] — fields: exterior_status, exterior_narrative (if affected)
IVR: "Exterior?"

Adjuster: "Not affected." — or — "North wall's smoke stained, spalling
above the garage, overhead door's warped, couple windows melted..."
  (system) — one exchange resolves the branch and, if affected, the
  elevation-by-elevation findings in the same breath.


8. DWELLING INSPECTION — INTERIOR
------------------------------------
[RECORD] — field: interior_damage_narrative [SKIP-OK]
IVR: "Interior?"

Adjuster: (free narration, or "nothing" / "clean")


9. PERSONAL PROPERTY
----------------------
[AIGATHER] — fields: personal_property_status, personal_property_narrative
IVR: "Personal property?"

Adjuster: "None." — or — "Kitchen cabinets have soot in them, on the
contents."
  (system) — the `damaged` branch always appends a forced
  "[NEEDS INPUT: confirm personal property list against the
  transcript]" flag to the rendered doc regardless of extraction
  confidence (README.md Phase 1, financial/inventory accuracy). Mention
  that once, in the read-back at the end — not on this call.


10. MITIGATION
---------------
[AIGATHER] — fields: mitigation_status, mitigation_narrative
IVR: "Mitigation vendor involved?"

Adjuster: "No." — or — "Board-up crew came the ninth, tarped the roof,
restoration's got scrubbers running in the kitchen and hall."
  (system) — `none` branch drops the MITIGATION heading entirely from
  the rendered doc, not left blank.


11. OVERHEAD & PROFIT
-----------------------
[RECORD] — field: overhead_profit_narrative
IVR: "Overhead and profit?"

Adjuster: (free narration)


12. SALVAGE & SUBROGATION
----------------------------
[RECORD] — field: subrogation_reason
IVR: "Any subrogation angle, or is this a dead end?"

Adjuster: (free narration, e.g. "weather related" / "ten year old
plumbing line, never repaired")


13. COINSURANCE
-----------------
[RECORD] — field: coinsurance_narrative
IVR: "Coinsurance?"

Adjuster: (free narration; "not applicable" is the answer on nearly
every claim — README.md notes 0 of 11 real filed reports carried a
coinsurance penalty)


14. READ-BACK & CLOSE
-----------------------
IVR: "That's everything. [reads back every REQUIRED field's captured
value, plus the personal-property confirmation flag if that branch was
taken]. Sound right?"

Adjuster: "Yep." — or — "No, redo [section]."
  → redo: jump back to that section's question, re-capture just that
    section
  → confirmed: "Good. Hang up whenever you're ready." Call ends.


WHAT HAPPENS IF THE ADJUSTER JUMPS AHEAD
--------------------------------------------------
Sections are a prompting/completeness boundary, not a data boundary.
If the adjuster is mid-answer on coverage and starts describing roof
damage, that audio still gets captured under whatever field is active
— nothing routes it live. Every section's transcript is stitched back
into one full, section-labeled transcript, and a single extraction
pass runs over the whole thing at the end of the call (same as today's
one-shot flow), so a stray comment recorded in the wrong section still
gets placed correctly afterward. See
docs/telnyx-texml-interactive-ivr.md, "Topic drift and cross-section
reconciliation." The adjuster should never feel like they have to hold
a thought until "the right section" — say it when it comes to mind.


OPEN QUESTIONS FOR BRANDON BEFORE BUILDING THIS
--------------------------------------------------
- Is a section-by-section walk (even this trimmed-down version)
  actually faster for him than the single continuous narration he does
  today, or does any stop-and-wait structure slow him down? The
  one-shot flow exists because he narrates fastest uninterrupted.
- roof_covering_type (7 values in enums.json) — genuinely closed set
  (roofing material is a small, well-known list) or free text with
  examples, same treatment as dwelling_type/siding_type? Decide before
  building section 6a's schema.
- Section 14's read-back — full read-back at the end, or confirm
  section-by-section as he goes (hear each answer parroted back
  immediately, correct it right there, move on) instead of one long
  list at the end of the call?
